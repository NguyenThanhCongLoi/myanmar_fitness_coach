# White FitnessUIUX and adaptive coach

Deployed to the existing free POC at https://avibigateway--myanmar-fitness-coach.modal.run . One CPU web container writes durable SQLite; the old app was stopped before deployment. Stable auth configuration was retained. Two additive migrations add visual body preferences and technique feedback. Paid TTS warm-up is disabled.

White surfaces, outlined cards, larger typography/icons and original illustrated intake follow the supplied reference. Green activity, cyan hydration, orange food/history and indigo strength are consistent across cards/charts. No commercial template code, logos or demo photos were copied.

Plans use actual completion and feedback, bounded weekly progression, equipment/protection constraints, daily time/lightness choices and explicit weekly check-in. Read refreshes cannot repeatedly increase difficulty. Missing nutrition logs do not count as low intake. Pain before any completed set is retained. Calendar opens actual session details. Rest/recovery continue to preserve streaks under existing protection rules.

Guided training has ten-second preparation, paused countdown/rest behind the exit sheet, large verified video/illustration, optional device speech, licensed music and form/RIR feedback. The production checklist covers 62 stable exercise IDs, including 26 without demo videos; upload owner-produced reviewed assets through the manifest described in VIDEO_PRODUCTION.md.
