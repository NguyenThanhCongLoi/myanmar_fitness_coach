CẦN TẠO VIDEO
Trình tự, setup và lỗi cần tránh
  1. Stand tall with the barbell resting on your upper back and hands holding it firmly.
  2. Step one foot back and lower your back knee towards the floor with control.
  3. Keep your torso upright and your front knee in line with your toes.
  4. Push through your front foot to return to standing, then switch legs.

Squat máy Smith

Smith machine squat

gym-smith-squat

Dụng cụ: Máy tập

Cơ chính: quadriceps; gluteal

Hai bên: Không yêu cầu clip tách bên

Có demo cần HLV duyệt
Trình tự, setup và lỗi cần tránh
  1. Set the safety stops just below your lowest squat position.
  2. Step under the bar, rest it on your upper back and place your feet slightly in front of you, shoulder-width apart.
  3. Twist the bar to unhook it, then bend your knees and hips to sit down slowly.
  4. Push through your whole foot to stand up, then twist the bar back onto the hooks when finished.

Always set the safety stops first. | Go only as low as you can keep your back straight.

Feet too far back so the knees hurt. | Forgetting to set the safety stops.
